# Audio Assets

Add your audio files here for the sound notifications.

## Required Files

- `authentic.mp3` - Sound played when verification is successful (used in SettingsContext)

## How to Add

1. Place your audio file(s) in this directory
2. The SettingsContext will load `authentic.mp3` when the sound feature is enabled

## Audio Guidelines

- Format: MP3 or WAV
- Duration: 300-500ms recommended (short beep/chime)
- Volume: Keep normalized to prevent distortion
